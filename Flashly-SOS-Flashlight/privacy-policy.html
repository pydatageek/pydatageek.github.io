Privacy Policy — Morse SOS Flashlight (Flashly)

Effective date: 2026-10-23
App: Morse SOS Flashlight ("Flashly"), package com.pydata.flashlysosflashlight
Developer: PyDataGeek ("we", "us")
Contact: pydatageekdev@gmail.com

This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • Two Google services built into the app collect some data: Google AdMob (to show banner ads) and Firebase Crashlytics (to report crashes).
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time.

2. Data collected by third-party services

The app itself sends no data to any server operated by us. The following Google services, included in the app, collect data directly:

Google AdMob (banner ads)

DataPurpose
Advertising IDAd delivery, frequency capping, measurement, and (only with consent where required) personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, and regional rules
Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)Ad measurement and fraud prevention
Basic device information (model, OS version, language)Showing ads that fit your device

Ads help keep the app free. Google acts as an independent controller for much of this data. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports, release builds only)

DataPurpose
Crash stack tracesFinding and fixing bugs
Device model, OS version, app versionReproducing the problem
Crashlytics installation UUIDCounting how many installs a crash affects (not linked to your identity)
TimestampsOrdering events around a crash

Crash reports are sent only when the app crashes. They do not contain your settings, Morse text, or anything you type. See Firebase privacy information.

The app does not use Firebase Analytics or any other usage-analytics service.

3. Consent and ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) asks whether you agree to personalised ads. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised (limited) ads.
  • Change your choice in the app: open Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices to see the consent form again and update your choice.
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Data stored only on your device

The app saves your preferences on your device using Android DataStore: flashlight brightness level, screen-light color and brightness, SOS light source, your custom Morse text, strobe speed, auto-off timer, and the shake-to-toggle setting.

This data is never sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, these preferences may be included in your device's Google backup (Android Auto Backup), which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this data.

The optional shake to toggle feature reads the accelerometer on the device only while the app is in use. Sensor data is processed locally and never leaves your device.

The app does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permissions. The flashlight uses Android's torch API, which does not need camera access.

6. Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Personalised ads: your consent, collected through the UMP form. You can withdraw it at any time (Section 3).
  • Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in funding a free app, and, where required, consent for storing or accessing information on your device.
  • Crash diagnostics: our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and safe to rely on in an emergency.

7. Data retention

  • Crashlytics: crash reports are kept by Firebase for about 90 days and then deleted automatically, according to Firebase's retention policy.
  • AdMob: retention is set by Google; see Google's retention policy.
  • On-device preferences: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold data that identifies you, we usually cannot link a request to you. Still, email us at pydatageekdev@gmail.com and we will help, including requesting deletion of crash data from Firebase where possible. For data held by Google, you can also use your Google Account and your device's ad settings.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will work to delete it.

10. Security

All data sent by AdMob and Crashlytics is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We do not run our own servers for the app.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
